About Decision Helper

Decision Helper is a web application meant assist in decision-making and reduce stress by helping break down choices into options, criteria, and weighted priorities.

How It Works

Users define their options, specify relevant criteria and assign them priority, then receive a ranked recommendation based off of the weighted scores.
